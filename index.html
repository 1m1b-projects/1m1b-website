Changemaker Chat

Catalyzing Impact with Manav Subodh

Q: What advice do you have for aspiring changemakers?

A: Start small, but start now. Focus on local problems and take action—courage and consistency matter more than scale.

Q: Which industries hold the most promise for tech-driven sustainability?

A: Renewable energy, sustainable agriculture, and waste management are leading the way. AI and data are making these industries smarter and greener.

Q: How has 1M1B evolved over time?

A: We've integrated AI, STEM, and sustainability. A major milestone is the Green Skills Academy, training 50,000 youth for clean energy jobs.
